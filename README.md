## Data Sources

### House of Commons Standing Committee on Health
The House of Commons Standing Committee on Health (HESA) documents can be found on the Parliament of Canada [website](ourcommons.ca/committees/en/HESA). The portal organizes records by parliamentary session, covering everything from witness transcripts to final committee reports.

HESA homepage, documents are grouped under several key tabs:

1. Meetings (Transcripts, Minutes & Webcasts)Under the Meetings tab, you will find chronological records of every committee session. For each meeting, you can access:

* Evidence: The full verbatim transcript (often called Hansard) of witness testimonies, presentations, and MP questions/debates.  
* Minutes: The formal summary of motions moved, votes taken, and official proceedings.
* Notice of Meeting: The agenda listing scheduled topics and appearing witnesses.
* Webcast / Video: Archival video or audio recordings of public broadcasts.  

2. Work (Studies, Briefs & Reports) Under the Work tab, documents are categorized by the specific study or legislative review the committee is conducting (e.g., Antimicrobial Resistance, Canada's Pharmaceutical Sovereignty, or specific health bills):

* Reports: Final substantive reports presented to the House of Commons, along with official Government Responses tabled by the Minister of Health (typically due within 120 days of a report).
* Written Briefs: Policy papers, data submissions, and recommendations submitted directly to the committee by external stakeholders, researchers, medical organizations, and advocacy groups during a study.

#### Navigating Past Parliaments

By default, the HESA portal displays documents for the current parliamentary session. If you are looking for historical documents, studies, or reports from previous years:
1. Locate the Parliament / Session drop-down menu near the top of the HESA homepage.
2. Select the relevant Parliament (e.g., 44th Parliament, 1st Session for 2021–2025).
3. The Meetings and Work tabs will automatically refresh to show archives from that specific period.
